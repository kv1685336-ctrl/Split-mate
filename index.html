About SplitMate

SplitMate is an expense-sharing web application designed to make managing money with friends, family members, and travel groups simple, organized, and transparent.

Users can organize shared expenses, calculate individual shares, track who paid each bill, and see suggested settlements.

Planned features include receipt uploads, item-by-item splitting, trip expense management, multiple currencies, and saved histories.

SplitMate calculates expense shares and suggests settlements. It does not automatically transfer money.

Secure accounts, receipt recognition, currency conversion, and permanent storage require additional technical services.

Rules for Using SplitMate

1. Account Usage

Keep your account credentials private.

2. Enter Accurate Information

Check member names, prices, and expense amounts.

3. Receipt Uploads

Review receipt details before relying on them.

4. Fair Bill Splitting

Assign expenses to the people responsible for sharing them.

5. Record Who Paid

Record the actual payer for each expense.

6. Trip Expenses

Avoid duplicate entries and include all trip expenses.

7. Currency

Check the currency used for each expense.

8. Settlement Results

Review suggested payments before settling balances.

9. Respect Other Members

Respect everyone's privacy and discuss disputes politely.

10. Protect Saved Information

Do not share sensitive information unnecessarily.

11. Check Before Confirming

Review calculations and expense details.

12. Use SplitMate Responsibly

Do not falsify expenses or access another person's account.

Our principle: Be honest, check calculations, and respect everyone's privacy.
