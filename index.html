About SplitMate

SplitMate is an expense-sharing web application designed to make managing money with friends, family members, and travel groups simple, organized, and transparent. It helps users organize shared expenses, calculate individual shares, track who paid each bill, and show who needs to pay whom. Users can create an account, log in, and access their dashboard to choose between Split a Bill and Create a Trip. With Split a Bill, users can add members, upload a receipt photo, or enter items and prices manually. Receipt recognition can help identify item names and prices, but users should review the results. Users can assign each item to the people who consumed it, divide shared items equally among selected members, and record who paid. With Create a Trip, users can organize expenses for holidays, outings, vacations, and other group activities. They can record food, accommodation, transportation, tickets, shopping, and custom expense categories. SplitMate is planned to support multiple currencies, saved bills and trips, expense histories, and settlement suggestions showing who owes whom. Optional invitations can let friends participate without requiring every member to create an account. SplitMate calculates expense shares and suggests settlements; it does not automatically transfer money. Receipt recognition, currency conversion, secure accounts, and saved information will require the appropriate technical services. The goal of SplitMate is simple: make sharing expenses fair, clear, convenient, and stress-free.

Rules for Using SplitMate

Welcome to SplitMate! Follow these guidelines to help everyone manage shared expenses fairly, accurately, and responsibly.

1. Account Usage

Create an account or log in before accessing saved bills and trips. Keep your password private and protect your account.

2. Enter Accurate Information

Enter correct item names, prices, expense amounts, member names, and payment details. Review information before saving.

3. Receipt Uploads

You may upload a receipt or enter items manually. Check recognized item names, quantities, prices, taxes, discounts, and totals.

4. Fair Bill Splitting

Assign each item to the people who consumed it. Shared items are divided equally among the selected members.

5. Record Who Paid

Record the person who actually paid each expense. The payer and the people responsible for sharing the expense may be different.

6. Trip Expenses

The organizer manages the member list and expense records. Avoid duplicate entries and check that all expenses are included.

7. Currency and Conversion

Select the correct currency for each expense and the trip's main currency. Check exchange rates and converted amounts carefully.

8. Review Settlement Results

Review suggested payments before settling balances. SplitMate calculates expenses but does not transfer money between users.

9. Respect Other Members

Discuss disputed expenses politely. Respect other people's privacy and obtain appropriate permission before sharing their information.

10. Protect Saved Information

Keep account credentials private and avoid uploading sensitive information that is unnecessary for expense calculations.

11. Check Before Confirming

Check receipt details, calculations, exchange rates, and settlement suggestions before relying on the results.

12. Use SplitMate Responsibly

Do not falsify expenses, misuse another person's account, or access information without permission.

Our principle: Be honest about expenses, check your calculations, and respect everyone's privacy.
